# Director's plan

## Story arc

1. **The signal:** A cyber-risk analyst notices a new warning. Caption: “AI is advancing quickly. Can the safeguards keep up?”
2. **The stakes:** Researchers follow careful containment procedures. Caption: “Powerful AI can amplify serious risks. Test before release.”
3. **The challenge:** Reviewers question an AI test result before launch. Caption: “Good intentions are not evidence. Ask an independent reviewer to challenge the evidence.”
4. **The human control:** A risk lead pauses an AI-assisted workflow and checks the alert with a colleague. Caption: “Monitor after launch. Keep a human ready to pause the system.”
5. **The close:** Palm92 asks what organisations should prove before high-risk AI goes live.

## Visual prompts (summarised)

- Vertical documentary shot of a London cyber-risk analyst noticing an unusual signal in a security operations centre; slow dolly, cool blue with restrained amber, no readable screen text or dialogue.
- Vertical documentary shot of researchers checking a sealed sample case behind a laboratory barrier; careful, calm, no open materials, no dialogue.
- Vertical documentary shot of an independent reviewer challenging a test result with an engineer; real office, charts without legible labels, no dialogue.
- Vertical documentary shot of a risk lead pausing an AI-supported workflow after an alert, then reviewing it with a colleague; no patient data, no dialogue.

The four eight-second Flow clips use a documentary visual style and short cross-dissolves. Palm92 captions and the closing card were added in post-production. The generated scenes are illustrative and do not depict actual organisations or incidents.
