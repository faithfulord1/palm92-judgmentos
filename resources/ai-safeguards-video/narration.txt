AI safeguards need evidence. A promise is not proof. Before high-risk AI goes live, ask: What was tested? Who checked it? Who owns the risk? Human judgement sets the purpose and limits. AI supports work within them. People verify important outputs and keep records. After launch, monitor outcomes, report incidents and improve controls. If safeguards fail, who can pause or roll back?
